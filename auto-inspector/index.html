Yen-Teng Chang / Projects

Auto Inspector — Automated Building Defect Inspection and Digital Twin System

Sinotech Engineering Consultants R&D contract · Sole developer · 2025–2026

  • Identified the lack of spatially anchored defect information in image-based inspection and developed an iPad-based system integrating LiDAR–IMU–RGB SLAM, RoomPlan, and AR raycasting.
  • Extended RoomPlan with custom ARKit mesh processing to reconstruct ceilings, beams, and other top-level geometry, enabling spatially constrained defect localization on reconstructed wall surfaces.
  • Anchored 125 crack instances across a large detention basin into a common 3D coordinate frame with <1.5% geometric error; a 16.7 cm field-measured crack was estimated as 16.9 cm.
  • Delivered a self-contained digital-twin inspection workflow generating 3D models, floor plans, inspection records, and coordinate-aware data, reducing inspection time by 85% and supporting field assessment with professional engineers.
