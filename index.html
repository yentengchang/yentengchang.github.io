Featured project · Sanlien Technology Corp.

Crack Monitoring System

A multi-camera Windows system that converts continuous crack imagery into measurements in physical units and event-linked records.

Sole developer · September 2025 – January 2026

Scroll-driven system walkthrough Scroll to advance ↓

Single-camera measurement

Start with the crack itself.

One camera view brings detection controls, calibration and measurement results into the same working interface.

Image correction

Correct the incoming frame first.

Camera parameters are applied before scale setup, segmentation or geometry measurement.

Physical scale

Connect pixels to real dimensions.

The checkerboard and its known square size establish the physical scale used by later measurements.

Segmentation

Isolate the crack as a region.

The segmentation result follows the crack geometry while retaining the detection label and confidence.

Length

Trace the centerline.

Skeletonization reduces the mask to its centerline so the estimated length can be recorded.

Maximum width

Search the full crack, then keep the widest point.

The distance field is evaluated along the mask before the maximum width is retained in the result.

Multi-camera monitoring

Extend the same workflow across camera streams.

The single-camera view becomes a coordinated monitoring wall for concurrent observation.

Seismic event recording

The record begins before the trigger.

A rolling buffer preserves the lead-up, then continues through and after the seismic event—organizing every camera stream under the same event record.
