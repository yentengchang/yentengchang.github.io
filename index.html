Featured project · Sanlien Technology Corp.

Crack Monitoring System

A multi-camera Windows system that converts continuous crack imagery into measurements in physical units and event-linked records.

Sole developer · September 2025 – January 2026

Interaction prototype · Home beats 1–2 Scroll to follow the system ↓

System overview

Continuous vision. One coordinated system.

Multiple camera streams are managed inside one desktop application, with connection and physical-scale states visible at a glance.

Monitoring wall

Every camera remains observable.

The overview expands into a monitoring wall without leaving the application or reducing each stream to a disconnected screenshot.

Camera detail

Move from system state to one inspection view.

Selecting a camera preserves its identity and status while bringing the processed frame and measurements into focus.

Image preparation

Corrected first. Scaled before inference.

Lens correction is applied before a checkerboard and known square size establish the physical scale used by the measurement pipeline.

Segmentation

The crack becomes a measurable region.

A segmentation mask follows the visible crack geometry instead of reducing the observation to a single bounding box.

Pixel geometry

Length follows the centerline. Width follows the distance field.

The mask is skeletonized for estimated length, while cross-sections reveal the location of maximum width.

Physical measurement

Pixels become millimeters.

The previously established scale converts pixel geometry into an estimated crack length and maximum width in physical units.
