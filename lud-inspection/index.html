Lock-up devices accommodate slow movement and restrain rapid movement between bridge components. Inspection must connect what is visible with physical measurements and the criteria behind a grade.

02 / RGB-D measurement

Depth gives the image
a physical scale.

The depth camera supplies distance, and its calibrated intrinsics map image locations into 3D. Surface area and pin separation use the same spatial foundation.

Illustrative readout
Affected area 1,860 cm²
Extent 42%

The photographs have no paired depth frames. The point lattice is a geometric schematic, and the measurement values are illustrative.

From pixels to surface area.

Align depth to the RGB image, reject unreliable depth samples and back-project the region. Local surface orientation accounts for viewing angle when estimating area; the applicable reference area defines the extent ratio.

Control the input,
the output and the hand-off.

Constraints make the assessment structured and auditable. They do not make every legal answer correct; the quality gate and engineer review remain separate decisions.

  1. Grounding by deterministic lookup

    Map the deterioration type to relevant standard rows. Only these candidate descriptions reach the model.

  2. Schema and enum constraints

    Constrain decoding to the required structure, permitted grade values and candidate row identifiers—not just a prompt asking for well-formed output.

  3. Deterministic validation

    Check required fields, permitted values and evidence references. E comes from geometric measurement and rules, rather than a model-generated estimate.

  4. Quality gate and provenance

    Route uncertain or inconsistent cases to the engineer. Retain the original output, applied rules, final decision and supporting evidence.
