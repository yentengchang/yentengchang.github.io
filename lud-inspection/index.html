Lock-Up Device (LUD) Intelligent Inspection Technology — Deterioration Segmentation and Code-Based Assessment

Technical proposal and system development · Sole developer · November 2024 – present

  • Investigated field inspection needs and defined an automated LUD deterioration-assessment workflow covering image acquisition, segmentation and classification, geometric measurement, and engineering assessment.
  • Established a standardized annotation and iterative model-development workflow with SAM-assisted labeling, hard-sample rebalancing, and human-reviewed cases incorporated as new ground truth.
  • Designed a constrained vision–language-model framework for DERU-based assessment (Degree / Extent / Relevancy / Urgency), integrating structured outputs, rule validation, consistency checks, confidence gating, and auditable decision logs.
  • Implemented human-in-the-loop feedback that returns reviewed low-confidence assessments to the system as retrieval knowledge.
