Technical contribution

Rotation Pose
Tracking

Turning recorded motion into pose histories.

I helped a labmate track the motion of a rotating isolation mechanism: placing ArUco markers, recording the experiment, running computer-vision pose tracking, and visualizing the results.

About the device

A sliding platform.
Two rotating disks.

The upper platform rides on rollers above two horizontal disks. L-shaped slots beneath the platform engage vertical pins positioned away from the disks’ rotation axes.

Relative sideways movement brings a pin against a slot wall, turning its disk. In the opposite direction, the other pin–slot pair engages. Accelerating or decelerating the disks requires torque, producing an inertial response through the linkage.

Recording & results

One recording. One time axis.

Watch the mechanism alongside its estimated rotation and translation. Play, pause, or scrub to examine the same instant in all three views.

Tracking method

Known geometry.
Frame-by-frame pose.

Four markers define a board on the disk. Their image corners, together with camera calibration and the measured geometry, provide the inputs for pose estimation.

  1. 01

    Calibrate the camera

    ChArUco observations establish the camera matrix and lens-distortion parameters.

  2. 02

    Match marker corners

    Detect ArUco IDs and associate their image corners with the disk’s known marker geometry.

  3. 03

    Estimate relative motion

    Solve the board pose, then express translation and rotation relative to a reference frame.

  4. 04

    Inspect the histories

    Export six pose channels and marker counts to CSV, then plot them against recording time.

Output

A record you can inspect.

The CSV contains X, Y, Z, RX, RY, RZ, and the number of visible markers for each frame. Here, RZ describes relative rotation about the disk normal; X follows the principal horizontal translation direction estimated from the trajectory.

These are vision-based pose estimates. Marker count provides context for visibility; it is not an accuracy score.
